Teaching

Contributing to education.

From primary school classrooms in the UK to university lecture halls in Milan, I enjoy sharing what I do with students at very different stages of learning.

In the past I ran sessions on UX and design thinking with primary school children in the UK, introducing them to the idea that the things they use every day are designed by someone, for someone.

More recently I have been cooperating with the University of Milan, running workshops for bachelor and master students on user-centred design, UX and the design process.

Community

A festival for inclusion.

I help organise a festival in my home town that celebrates inclusion.

It brings local people, musicians, professionals and organisations together to celebrate families and diversity. I lend a hand with marketing, communications and coordination on the event.

Illustration

I illustrated a children’s book.
